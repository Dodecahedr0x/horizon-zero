Past the last road the earth is ash-grey and the light burns ember. The dead rose from the burned city as the Ashborn, the last grove walked out as the Rootborn, and the Deeplings crawled up from the drowned harbor. All three want the same ground.

The match

Three moves. After the third, nobody touches the fight.

  1. I

    Lock an army

    Pick a people, then spend the battleground budget on up to ten of its soldiers. Set each one on a tile in your starting zone. The roster closes when you lock it.

  2. II

    Stake SOL

    Outpost, Garrison, or Citadel. The stake sits with the army for the match.

  3. III

    Auto-battle

    A VRF seed lays rubble on the open ground between the zones and resolves the fight, round by round, to the last soldier standing or round twelve.

The field

Each battleground is a grid. One army starts on the top two rows, the other on the bottom two. The seed decides where the rubble falls between them. It never cuts one zone off from the other.
