A Thornling: the smallest Rootborn, still taller than a man, its whole body woven from countless thin, wiry, needle-sharp thorn stems and bramble tendrils twisted tightly together like braided wire, gaps of ember-orange glow showing between the strands, long whip-like thorn arms ending in clusters of spikes, a spiky bramble crown, a lean, forward, aggressive stance. No thick trunk, no bark slabs.
