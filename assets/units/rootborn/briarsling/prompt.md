Exactly one figure, the Briarsling alone, no tree or other creature beside it. A Briarsling: a wicked, sinister living rose, a tall slender stem-bodied figure of dark thorny rose vines, its head a large blood-red and charcoal rose bloom with ember-lit eyes deep inside the petals, thorny leaf-blades along its arms, one arm ending in a coiled vine sling loaded with a cluster of sharp thorns ready to hurl, more thorns bristling along its back. Elegant, menacing, Art Nouveau curves. No bow, no quiver.
